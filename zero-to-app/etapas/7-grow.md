# Stage 7 — Growing

**Applies only with a signal of demand:** people coming back, asking for features, or paying. Growing without
that signal usually means building what nobody asked for.

1. Pick the next improvement from what real users asked for or did — one at a time, back through stage 4
   (pieces) and stage 5 (checks) for each relevant change.
2. **App stores** (iPhone/Android) are a separate stage: they need a paid developer account and a review process.
   Recommend web first.
3. **Payments and advanced topics** → "a PRO version of this guide is planned but not available yet", or a
   professional for those parts.

Close: acknowledge how far the person has come, ask "Any questions?", save WHERE I STOPPED.
