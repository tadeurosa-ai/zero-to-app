# Stage 6 — Own domain

**Applies only when there is a real customer or user.** Before that, the free address is enough — a paid domain
doesn't prove the idea.

1. Explain what a domain is (the address, like mysite.com) and that it is rented by the year.
2. Show the yearly cost **and the renewal cost** (often higher than the first year), plus taxes and fees.
3. Email at that address and DNS settings (the "address book" that points the domain to the app) — explain them
   only when they become needed.
4. Buy or configure only with an explicit "yes".
5. After connecting: open the new address and check it loads the published app. A new address is a relevant
   change: redo the not-logged-in and account tests from stage 5 on it.

Close: acknowledge the progress, ask "Any questions?".
